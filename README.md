### 🖋️ AR Tattoo Try-On
See a tattoo on your skin before getting inked. Draw a small marker, point your phone camera, and the design sticks to your arm. Upload your own design and remove its background, then pinch and twist to place it. Runs in the browser, nothing is uploaded. Based on my AR work for our capstone project, MAGI-INK.

**Built with:** JavaScript · YOLOv8 (ONNX Runtime Web) · OpenCV.js · WebGL · Vercel

▶ [Try it live](https://tattoo-tryon-chi.vercel.app)
